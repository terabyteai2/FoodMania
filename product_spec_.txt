 QuickBytes is a mobile-first POS for small/medium restaurants. It ingests orders from **5 channels** (website storefront, Messenger chatbot, table-QR, waiter, and most importanly, counter, ) into one queue with a strict **pending → accepted → completed** lifecycle. It runs the floor (FOH, dine-in/parcel/delivery), manages menu(delivery charge according to area, discounting, online delivery availability), inventory (with stock-in, counts), a MOAT AI scan- which scans a menu card and creates the entired menu in 10 seconds (updates and changes existing menus also), AI scan in the inventory also scans handwritten supplier bills and handwriten end of day counts to input everything very accurately into the right fields, the backend automatically does inventory calculations, and slowly through pattern recognition, gives alerts such as low stock. the app also  surfaces owner **analytics**, lets an owner/manager **take over Messenger chats** the bot can't handle, and exposes **staff, audit, and settings**.there are roles and role based features into the unified app, owner (analytics home screen,order history, inventory, menu, settings ), manager (orders home screen, plus button FOH, set bulk disccounts, item availability, online delivery availablity, counter open/close, day sales summary, [printer settings), and waiter (FOH home screen, created orders).
