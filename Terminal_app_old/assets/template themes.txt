

Theme 1 · Napoli Trattoria (Pizza)
The current baseline, tuned. Naples back-alley trattoria — rustic, warm, a little theatrical.

Palette: Vesuvio red #C8392E, charcoal #1A1614, basil #4A6B3A, parchment cream #F2E8D5, the existing amber as accent
Type: Anton or Bebas Neue (condensed, all-caps for the brand name) + Cormorant Garamond italic for tagline and dish names
Hero: Keep the video full-bleed. Dark gradient from bottom. Logo medallion gets a hand-drawn laurel or pizza-paddle motif inside instead of just the letter. Coral CTA → swap to deep Vesuvio red, dark text.
Menu: Vertical list, no images per row by default (Italian menus are typographic). Dish name in serif italic, "wood-fired · 12 min" small-caps subline, price right-aligned with ৳ in a smaller superscript. A BASIL · BUFALA · TOMATO tag row in tiny tracked caps.
Signature: A thin, dotted-line divider between dishes that looks like flour dusted on a counter. Section headings in handwritten chalk-style font ("La Pizza", "I Dolci").


Theme 2 · Tuscan Herb (Pasta)
Slower, softer, more domestic. Nonna's kitchen, not a restaurant.

Palette: Sage #8FA382, terracotta #C97A5A, olive black #2C2A1F, parchment #F4ECDC, deep wine #5A1F26 accent
Type: Fraunces or Lora (editorial serif, mixed-case — not all-caps) for brand name + a hand-script (Caveat, Petit Formal Script) for accents like "since 1987"
Hero: Pasta video gets a warm sepia-ish grade, a torn-paper bottom edge above the menu, brand name in italic serif mixed-case ("Ebrahim" not "EBRAHIM"). CTA becomes a flat sage rectangle with a small fork-and-spoon icon, no pill.
Menu: Two-column on wider phones, recipe-card feel. Each dish: serif italic name, one-line ingredient list in muted text ("hand-rolled fettuccine, pecorino, black pepper, guanciale"), price in a small tag. Categories as left-aligned section headers with a thin sage rule underneath.
Signature: A tiny botanical line-drawing (rosemary sprig, olive branch) at every category header. Page corners feel like a cookbook page.


Theme 3 · Amalfi Breeze (Lemon-Mint Mocktail / drinks-led café)
Coastal, summery, breezy. The opposite of heavy.

Palette: Mediterranean blue #2E6F8E, lemon #F5D547, mint #9DD4B5, paper white #FBFAF5, soft coral #E89A8A accent
Type: A semi-display sans like General Sans or Söhne for the brand + a playful italic display (Recoleta, Boska) for headers
Hero: Video is bright, high-key, condensation glass. Subtle moving "frosted glass" overlay panel where the logo sits — a backdrop-blur rectangle on top of the video, not a circle medallion. CTA is a tilted-slightly white pill with blue text.
Menu: Drinks-first layout. Each card has a backdrop-blur (frosted glass) effect with the dish image faintly behind. "Tasting notes" line in small caps: SWEET · CITRUS · HERBAL. Categories scroll horizontally as round chips with a lemon-slice icon.
Signature: Lemon-slice dot patterns as section dividers. Hover/tap on a drink card → tiny ripple animation like ice in a glass.


Theme 4 · Milano Roast (Coffee / specialty café)
Minimal, sharp, Italian café-book. The most editorial of the eight.

Palette: Espresso #3B2A1F, crema #E8D5B7, brass #B89556, off-white #FAF7F2, near-black #15110D
Type: Futura or GT America (geometric sans, tracked wide) for brand + JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono for prices and origin labels
Hero: Espresso pour video gets a heavy vignette. Brand name appears small, centered, tracked wide in caps — not huge. A thin brass horizontal rule above and below. CTA is a thin-bordered rectangle, not filled — restrained.
Menu: Looks like a real coffee shop chalkboard menu. Single column. Dish name left, leader dots · · · · · · filling the line, price right in monospace. Origin/process line below in italic: Ethiopia · Yirgacheffe · washed. Categories are simple uppercase labels with a thin rule, no boxes.
Signature: Monospace pricing throughout. A small brass coffee-bean glyph as the "available" indicator vs a hollow circle for "sold out".


Theme 5 · Sultan's Hearth (Kebab)
Ottoman / Mughal opulence, ember and gold. Built for dimly-lit ambience photography.

Palette: Deep burgundy #5C1A1B, charcoal #1A1410, antique gold #C9A24B, ember orange #E26B2C, ivory #F0E5D0
Type: Cinzel or Trajan (Roman/Ottoman feel) for English + Hind Siliguri bold for Bangla, treated co-equally. A decorative arabesque-style display for the brand mark itself.
Hero: Flame-kissed skewer video, deep dark grade, ember glow leaking from edges. Logo medallion gets a gold filigree border ring (8-pointed star or arabesque). Brand name in gold, Bangla version below in equal size. CTA is a gold-bordered dark rectangle with gold text.
Menu: Each dish gets a small gold-rule frame. Bilingual title (English + Bangla stacked or side-by-side), spice meter as 1–3 small flame icons in ember orange, "served with naan, salad & raita" italic subline. Categories are ornate centered headers with arabesque flourishes left and right.
Signature: Arabesque corner ornaments on cards. Section dividers are not lines but a centered 8-point star motif. This theme handles Bangla type with the most dignity — important for Bengali kebab houses.


Theme 6 · The Charcoal Lodge (Grill / steak)
Masculine, smoky, butcher-shop confidence. Less ornament than Sultan's, more industrial.

Palette: Deep charcoal #1C1C1E, smoke gray #5C5852, blood red #8B2828, brass #A0814D, bone white #E8E2D4
Type: Playfair Display Black or Ogg (heavy editorial serif) for brand + a stenciled or industrial mono (Space Mono, Major Mono) for weights and prices
Hero: Sear-mark close-up video, smoke rising. Brand name in stamped/embossed style — solid block serif, maybe with a subtle inner-shadow to look pressed into leather. CTA is a hard-edge red square, no pill, no rounding.
Menu: Like a butcher's slate. Dark surface for the menu section (not light). Dish name in white serif, weight in monospace · 280g ·, doneness chips (RARE · MED · WELL), price in big white numerals. Sear-cross icon for spice/heat level.
Signature: The whole menu section background is dark even if the rest of the site is light. A subtle paper-grain texture overlay. Hairline dividers in brass, not gray.


Theme 7 · Gourmet Bengal Bistro (Fuchka / elevated street food)
This is your most locally-relevant theme. Modern Bengali bistro — street food taken seriously, plated like a magazine.

Palette: Bone white #F5F2EB, indigo #2C3E66, mustard #D9A431, mint chutney green #7BA88C, deep tamarind #6B3825 accent
Type: Fraunces or Tiempos (modern editorial serif) for English + Hind Siliguri given equal visual weight — this is the theme that puts Bangla on the front foot. Headers can stack: Fuchka / ফুচকা.
Hero: Overhead macro plate video, white background, almost food-magazine. Brand name in serif mixed-case, centered. Below: a smaller Bangla version in elegant Hind Siliguri. Mustard CTA pill with deep indigo text.
Menu: Magazine layout. Each dish card has a square plated image, dish name in serif (Bangla underneath smaller), price formatted as ৳ 80 / 6 pcs with the qty in muted text, a 1–3 chili "spice meter", and a tiny tag for street favorite or chef's pick. Categories as a horizontal pill row in deep indigo.
Signature: The bilingual stacked typography is the move. A small mustard "selected dot" under the active category. This is the theme a hip Dhanmondi or Banani fuchka spot picks.


Theme 8 · The Grand Mughal (Biryani)
The opulent counterpart to Sultan's Hearth. Where Sultan's is ember and gold, Grand Mughal is saffron and pearl. Court, not hearth.

Palette: Royal aubergine #3A1F3D, saffron #E8A33D, pearl ivory #F4EEDF, antique gold #B8923A, deep maroon #5E1F2A
Type: Cormorant SC or Trajan for English (regal, Roman-inscriptional) + Hind Siliguri in a more calligraphic weight. The brand mark could be wordmark-only, no medallion — just the name in gold on aubergine.
Hero: Saffron strands falling on basmati rice slow-mo, or a copper handi being uncovered with steam rising. Heavy gold-leaf frame around the entire hero. Brand name in saffron on aubergine, gold rule underneath. CTA in saffron with deep aubergine text, rectangular with subtle gold inner border.
Menu: Manuscript-style. Wide outer margins (the menu doesn't go edge-to-edge — it has gold rule frames). Each dish: name in serif title-case, descriptor line slow-cooked · 4 hours · serves 2, price in elegant numerals. Categories are centered serif headers with gold flourishes on either side.
Signature: The framed-page feel — the menu literally looks like a leaf from a royal cookbook. Hero dishes (kacchi, tehari) get a "royal favorite" gold seal stamp.
