4. RISK NOTES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. INK COLOR CHANGE (14180E → 1A1E14): This shifts the primary text color from
     pure-black-green to a slightly softer ink. All text in the app will shift.
     The change is subtle (~3% lighter) but pervasive.

  2. ACCENT-STRONG (498F18 → 3E7E14): Used for lime text on white backgrounds.
     Changing this affects text buttons, links, and active text indicators. The
     new value is slightly darker/bluer — verify legibility.

  3. DESKTOP POS SIDE-EFFECTS: The Pc theme aliases PosColors values. When we
     update PosColors.background (F3F5EE → F7F8F4), the desktop module's Pc.bg
     changes too. Either: (a) pin Pc values to their old literals, or (b) accept
     the visual shift on desktop.

  4. SECTION HEADING SHRINK (18px → 15px): TfSectionHeader and titleLarge usage
     throughout the app will visually shrink. Screens with tight layouts will
     gain breathing room; screens designed around 18px may look sparse.
