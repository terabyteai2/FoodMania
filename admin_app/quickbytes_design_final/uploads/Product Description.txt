Quickbytes Overview & Core Capabilities
Quickbytes is a mobile restaurant POS featuring a 3-step dine-in/takeaway flow via dedicated Manager and Waiter apps.
* Hardware & Storefront: Supports Bluetooth/USB receipt and kitchen printing, table-based QR codes, and a customized online storefront with online order processing.
* Chatbot: Online facebook chatbot automatically takes orders and prints out receipt with order details.
* Menu & Inventory Management: AI-powered menu scanning with auto-filled categorization. Menu controls include add/edit/toggle availability, discounts, AI inventory scanning, stock-in autofill, start count fields, and variance tracking.
* Reporting & Security: Generates daily/weekly reports, owner dashboard analytics, role management, and offline backup.
The Complexity Dial Concept
Instead of restrictive tier-based pricing models that fragment database schemas, Quickbytes utilizes a single unified data model (using nullable fields) and one codebase. The Complexity Dial allows owners to scale visibility and UI complexity dynamically as their business grows, without changing software platforms.
The Dial: Four Positions
Position 1 — SIMPLE
* Target: Juice bars, tea stalls, fuchka carts, counter-service only (single operator, no multi-user attribution, no floor maps, no scheduling).
* Dashboard: Earned today, payment breakdown (cash/card/online), quick-sale buttons, top movers, and a "Close day" action.
* Inventory: Stock value, used today vs. average, top items by movement, and add stock / start count actions.
* Front of House (FOH): Direct order entry (automatic "counter" mode, no table selection), receipt printing only.
Position 2 — STANDARD
* Target: Small dine-in (5–10 tables), 2–3 staff, 1 manager (floor management and shift attribution begin).
* Dashboard (Manage): Earned today, 4-column grid floor map, kitchen status (order count, oldest, late orders), table status snapshots, "Alarts" alerts (late orders, low stock, stockouts), shift strip (current shift, staff name, end button), and top movers. Quick actions: New order, print bill, check inventory, settings.
* Dashboard (Review): Today vs. yesterday comparison, 24-hour hourly revenue bar chart, source breakdown, and top ranked items.
* Inventory: 3 KPI cards (value, health), filtered item list (on-hand, movement, status), and add stock / start count buttons.
* FOH: Order entry with table selection, waiter floor map, order modification, and shift attribution (waiter tagging).
* Staff: Active shift logs (who is logged in, clock-in times) and end-shift button.
Position 3 — ADVANCED
* Target: Full-service dine-in (15–40 tables), 5–8 staff, distinct owner and manager roles (adds operational efficiency, profit metrics, and costings).
* Dashboard (Manage): Expanded 6-column scrollable floor map, kitchen station breakdowns (appetizers, mains, desserts), expanded table statuses, expanded alerts (long-seated tables, bottlenecks), top movers with profit margins, expanded shift strip (total covers, average ticket, staff count). Quick actions add: Floor settings, view menu, staff scoreboard.
* Dashboard (Review): 7-day trend line charts with ghost bars, top items with food cost % and contribution margins, profitability 4-quadrant matrix (Star, Steady, Puzzle, Review items), covers, average ticket, table turns, and a staff scoreboard (covers, average ticket, service time per waiter).
* Inventory: Value + health + food cost %, linked menu items costing, daily reconciliation variance tracking, and 7-day usage reorder suggestions.
* FOH: Table + waiter selection, kitchen-sent markers for modified orders, item substitutions, dietary tags, and comp/discount reason tracking.
* Staff: Full scoreboard metrics (covers, ticket size, speed, comps, mistakes, earnings) and mandatory shift handover flows for open tables.
Position 4 — ENTERPRISE
* Target: Multi-location fleets (2–10 outlets), 40+ tables per outlet, multi-manager hierarchies.
* Dashboard (Manage): Multi-outlet view (read-only for owner, localized management view for managers).
* Dashboard (Review): Fleet KPIs (total revenue, covers, outlet rankings), outlet-to-outlet comparisons (revenue, covers, avg ticket, food cost %, late %, waste %, variance), fleet-wide trend lines, aggregate top items, cross-location waiter efficiency, capacity utilization, and AI forecasting.
* Inventory: Consolidated fleet stock value, fleet top movers, variance by location, bulk purchase order reorder suggestions, and drill-down stock breakdowns per location.
* FOH: Standard Advanced view with active outlet-switching tabs.
* Staff: Fleet-wide directory, 2-week lookahead calendar shift scheduling, AI capacity/staffing recommendations, and cross-outlet staff transfers.
